# ADR 0018: No time estimates and no schedule tracking

Date: 2026-10-27. Status: accepted. Replaces the durations in `docs/PLAN.md` and in ADRs 0016
and 0017.

**Context.** The plan gave every phase a duration in weeks, and ADRs 0016 and 0017 changed some
of them. Greg works on the project at his own pace, with agents, and a phase may take a day or a
month; the numbers were guesses that nobody used to decide anything. The options were to keep
the estimates and update them as phases finish, or to drop them.

**Decision.** Drop them, chosen by Greg on 2026-10-27. Phases keep their order, scope, tests and
done criteria, and carry no duration. Specs, reports and plans do not estimate time or track a
schedule. The week figures in ADRs 0016 and 0017 no longer apply; the rest of those decisions
stands.
